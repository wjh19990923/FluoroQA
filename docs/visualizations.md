# Visualizations and provenance

## Overview and local-calibration diagrams

`assets/overview.svg` and `assets/local-calibration.svg` are original vector
schematics made for this repository. They contain no medical images, patient
metadata or numerical model predictions. They illustrate the code's data flow
and the current-pose neighborhood geometry. They are covered by the repository's
MIT license. The neighborhood ring is a conceptual layout, not a 6D pose plot.

## Aggregate result chart

`assets/results.svg` is generated by `scripts/build_results_figure.py` from the
12 public aggregate rows in `assets/results.json`. Those rows were checked
against the independently reviewed 2026-10-21 classification comparison.
Only model names, cohort type, checkpoint-selection policy, sample counts,
accuracy and pooled AUROC are copied. No individual score, image, pose, patient
identifier or private filesystem path is released.

This is the 256-pixel experiment at the 2 mm success boundary. Raw uses a train
maximum-balanced-accuracy threshold; Local uses the historical validation
maximum-balanced-accuracy threshold and the current-pose 12-neighbor mean.
The exact 200-epoch checkpoint selections and 350-epoch terminal model differ.
The chart cannot isolate BCE from longer training and unfreezing. Traditional
baselines are omitted from this illustration, not claimed to be inferior.
All chart axes start at zero; intervals are not shown. Displayed values are
rounded, while the JSON retains the audited precision.

## Interactive companion

Website: https://jinhaolab.com/tools/2d3dreg/

Checked 2026-10-22: example loading, current projection and pose controls are
present. The page states that it does not perform automatic registration and
uses cropped/flipped/downsampled examples with adjusted camera calibration.
It is not presented here as a deployed FluoroQA model. User-data import was not
tested or used in this documentation check. Medical examples stay on the
companion website and retain its displayed CC BY-NC 4.0 license and source
attribution; they are not relicensed by this repository's MIT license.
